Manage and Secure Google Sheets Effortlessly

Googlemarks is a Google Sheets Add-on designed to streamline workspace workflows, batch-manage sheet properties, consolidate data across tabs, and lock individual sheets with 5-digit passcodes.

📁 Sheet Manager

Search, switch to, batch hide, unhide, duplicate, or clean up unused tabs across large workbooks instantly from a dedicated sidebar.

📊 Sheet Combiner

Select multiple sheets and combine their contents into a single consolidated master tab with automatic column matching.

🔒 Passcode Access Control

Protect individual sheets with 5-digit passcodes. Authorized non-owner collaborators can enter passcodes directly via the sidebar to access edit permissions with auto-lock timeouts.

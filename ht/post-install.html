Post-Install Setup Guide

1. Launching the Add-on

Open any Google Spreadsheet and click top menu: Extensions → Googlemarks → Open Googlemarks.

2. Lock Controls (Owner Only)

Only the Spreadsheet Owner can create new passcodes. Passcodes are recorded on a dedicated hidden sheet titled passcodes.

3. Automatic Lock Expiration

Unlocked sheets remain open for editing for 15 minutes before automatically re-locking via background triggers.
